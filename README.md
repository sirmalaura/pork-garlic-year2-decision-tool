# Year 2 Winter Decision Tool

Interactive comparison of two Year 2 winter strategies for Pork & Garlic Ice Cream Co.

## Features

- Confirmed Year 1 figures from the accounting workbook
- Editable Option A and Option B assumptions
- Sales-allocation validation
- Profit and loss and cash-flow projections
- Negative cash before the market warning
- Side-by-side decision comparison
- Writable decision and key-assumption notes

The live deployment is available at https://pork-garlic-year2-decision-tool.vercel.app/
